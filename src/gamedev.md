## Solo Projects

- **Fevered World (UT bacherlor's thesis)**

  City-building strategy game. The goal (and research object) was to
  create player opponents which acted sensibly, and gave the feeling of
  a "living world". This was attempted through implementing a "utility theory" AI.

  I was the game designer, developer, artist, market researcher. Made with Godot engine
  and C# programming language.

  [Thesis](https://thesis.cs.ut.ee/a02d0283-9f30-4df1-aba3-ec4613ebfe95){target="_blank"}
